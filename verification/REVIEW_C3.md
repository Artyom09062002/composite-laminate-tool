# C3: independent review of `core/dome.py` — verification of each comment

Each comment was checked by derivation or by a numerical test before any change was made (scripts were run against the code; the
checks that matter are now regression tests in `tests/test_dome.py`). Only confirmed defects were fixed; **no equation in
`core/dome.py` was changed**.

| # | Reviewer's label | Verdict | Evidence | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Error: polar opening vs pole | **Confirmed, wording only** | The maths is right and already excludes r0 <= 0 (tests: invalid opening radius). Two places said "toward the pole" (dome.py docstring, app text). | Docstring now states that r0 is the turnaround circle / opening radius, that the dome is truncated there (no geometric pole) and that r0 = 0 is excluded; app text corrected. |
| 2 | Questionable: thickness law | **Rejected as an error** | Flux argument gives t r cos(alpha) = const; closed form `t/t_cyl = sqrt((R²-r0²)/(r²-r0²))` matches it at r = 0.1, 0.08, 0.06, 0.052 m (R = 0.1, r0 = 0.05) and is already tested. `dome_stations` already rejects any ply not at ±alpha0, so t_cyl is the helical thickness. | None. The assumptions (constant band width and fibre volume, no slip) were already listed in the model limits. |
| 3 | Error: divergence at r0 used as a prediction | **Partly confirmed** | The code never evaluates r0 itself (`thickness_factor` returns inf; stations stop at 1.02 r0). But the headline "weakest station" picked a turnaround-zone station: R = 100 mm, r0 = 50 mm, 16 plies, k = 0.7 gave r = 55.9 mm (1.118 r0). | `DomeStations.membrane_valid` flags stations within one bending decay length of the junction or of the opening; `weakest_valid_index()` ignores them; the app headline, chart and text use it. |
| 4 | Stylistic: ellipsoid radii | **Rejected (no defect)** | r1, r2 agree with independently computed curvature of the parametric curve (finite differences, k = 0.5, 0.8, 1.3; 9 points; also the existing analytic test to 1e-12). Equator (b²/R, R) and pole (R²/b) limits checked. | None. |
| 5 | Stylistic: pressure membrane equations | **Rejected (no defect)** | Normal equilibrium N_phi/r1 + N_theta/r2 = p and axial balance 2 pi r N_phi sin(phi) = p pi r² hold at 9 stations (k = 0.5, 1.0, 1.3). Sphere and cylinder limits tested. Resultants are tensile-positive throughout. | None. |
| 6 | No error: compression criterion | **Agreed** | N_theta,eq / pR = 1 - 1/(2k²) for k = 0.5, 0.6, 0.7071, 0.9 (to 1e-15); zero crossing at Q/R² = 0.49999; both resultants -> pR²/(2b) at the pole. | None. |

| 8 | Questionable: helical-only dome is not a netting solution | **Rejected as an error** | The module uses CLT with the matrix, not netting equilibrium, and says so. | 10 | Error: hoop discontinuity at the junction | **Confirmed (reporting)** | Dome N_theta at the junction is pR(1 - 1/(2k²)): 0.5 pR (k = 1), -0.02 pR (k = 0.7), -pR (k = 0.5) against pR in the cylinder. For k = 1.0 and 0.5 the lowest first-ply pressure along the dome was exactly the junction station (3.42 MPa and 1.98 MPa), i.e. a membrane-jump artefact presented as a strength. | Same fix as item 3: the junction zone is flagged and excluded from the headline. The zone length (pi/beta, beta = (3(1-nu²))^(1/4)/sqrt(R h), nu = 0.3, cylinder wall thickness) is an isotropic thin-shell order-of-magnitude estimate, not a result for the helical wall; for a transition value use a shell-bending or finite-element analysis. |

Open point: the edge-zone estimate is an assumption (isotropic shell, cylinder wall thickness, which understates the zone at the
opening). With thick walls or shallow domes no station remains valid (the app then shows "n/a" instead of a number).
